Who We Are

Cool Treats, Warm Hearts.

At H-ICE, we believe that ice cream is more than just dessert — it's a vibe.

Born from a love of creativity, color, and coolness, H-ICE started with a simple goal: to turn classic ice cream flavors into a full-on experience. Every scoop we serve is a mix of playful design, nostalgic taste, and fresh, feel-good energy.

It all started with the idea of mixing heat (H) and ice — a balance of warmth and chill. From our pastel palettes to our mellow mood, H-ICE is a place for joy, comfort, and little moments of delight.
