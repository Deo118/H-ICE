Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2025

At H-ICE, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

  1. Information We Collect
    • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
    • Billing and shipping addresses
    • Payment details (processed securely via third-party platforms)
    • Website usage data (via cookies and analytics tools)
  2. How We Use Your Information
    • Fulfill and deliver your ice cream orders
    • Improve our website and customer experience
    • Send order updates or promotional content (if subscribed)
  3. Cookies

    We use cookies to enhance your experience. These help us remember your preferences and track usage data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

  4. Data Sharing

    We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share it with trusted third-party services (e.g., payment processors, delivery platforms) only to fulfill your orders.

  5. Security

    We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no online system is 100% secure, so use the site at your discretion.

  6. Your Rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at: email@example.com.

Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2025

We want you to love your experience with H-ICE. Due to the perishable nature of our products, we offer a limited refund policy as described below.

  1. Eligibility for Refunds
    • Your order arrives melted, damaged, or is incorrect
    • You contact us within 24 hours of delivery with proof (photo and order number)
  2. Non-Refundable Situations
    • Change of mind
    • Delivery delays caused by incorrect address entry or carrier issues outside our control
    • Opened or partially consumed products
  3. How to Request a Refund

    Please email us at hicecreamz@gmail.com with:

    • Your name
    • Order number
    • Description and photo of the issue
    Our support team will review your request and respond within 48 hours.

  4. Exchanges

    We do not offer exchanges due to the frozen nature of our products. However, in case of a valid issue, we may offer a coupon for a future order.

Contact Us
